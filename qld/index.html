How Queensland is mapped

Queensland describes its native vegetation as Regional Ecosystems (REs) — combinations of a bioregion, a land zone and a vegetation community. The pre-clear layer maps the REs that existed before clearing at 1:100,000, with finer 1:50,000 and 1:25,000 mapping in more populated country, so in parts of South East Queensland and the Wet Tropics it is finer than Victoria's EVC mapping.

Reading the RE code

An RE code like 12.3.11 reads as bioregion 12 · land zone 3 · vegetation unit 11. The middle number is the land zone — the underlying geology and landform — which already tells you a great deal about the country before any lookup: land zone 3 is alluvial river flats, land zone 8 is fertile basalt, land zone 1 is tidal saltmarsh.

Species from REDD

The Queensland Herbarium's Regional Ecosystem Description Database (REDD) holds a species-bearing description for every one of the ~1,600 regional ecosystems, openly licensed (CC-BY). We're folding those into planting lists — search your address and leave your email to get the species list for your RE.
