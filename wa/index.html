How Western Australia is mapped

Western Australia's pre-European vegetation was mapped by John Beard, whose 1:250,000 vegetation survey of the whole state was digitised and published as Beard et al. (2013). It's the only genuinely statewide pre-European layer for WA, and it's what we read for every WA address.

What association level means

At 1:250,000 the answer is a broad vegetation association, not a garden-scale community — for a suburban Perth block you'll get the dominant structure and species of the wider landscape rather than a precise site type. It's coarser than the community-level mapping in NSW, Victoria and Queensland, but far more specific than the national subgroups, and it's genuinely pre-European.

Species in the description

Usefully, Beard's mapping names the dominant species in each unit — so a Darling Range result returns not just “jarrah and marri” but Eucalyptus marginata and Corymbia calophylla. We surface those species directly on your result, and are building fuller indigenous planting lists on top.
