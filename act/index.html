How the ACT is mapped

The ACT doesn't publish its own pre-1750 vegetation layer, so we read the national NVIS 7.0 pre-1750 layer. Canberra sits in temperate grassy woodland and montane forest country — much of it box-gum grassy woodland, one of the most cleared and most threatened communities in south-eastern Australia.

A note for border addresses

The ACT is surrounded by New South Wales, which is mapped in far more detail. If your interest is just over the border, search the NSW address directly — you'll get a community-level Plant Community Type rather than a national subgroup.

Toward a planting list

We're building indigenous planting lists on top of the national subgroups. Search your address and leave your email to be sent the list for your vegetation when it's ready.
