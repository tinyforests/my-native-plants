How South Australia is mapped

South Australia has detailed pre-European floristic mapping, but only across the agricultural region — the cleared, settled crescent from the South East through the Mount Lofty Ranges, Adelaide and Yorke Peninsula to the Eyre Peninsula. Through the pastoral and arid two-thirds of the state, that layer stops.

Where the state layer stops, the national layer takes over

Outside the agricultural region — and at times when the state's mapping service is unavailable — we read the national NVIS 7.0 pre-1750 layer instead, and tell you which one you're seeing. Either way you get an honest, sourced answer rather than a blank.

Reading the vegetation type

South Australia's pre-European types are structured to the national NVIS hierarchy, which means the dominant species are embedded in the vegetation-type description. We surface what the mapping gives, and are building indigenous planting lists on top.
