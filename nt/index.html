How the Northern Territory is mapped

The Northern Territory does not publish a pre-1750 vegetation layer as an open web service, so we read the national NVIS 7.0 pre-1750 layer — the Major Vegetation Subgroups that describe the landscape as it was before European settlement across tropical savanna, spinifex sandplain, floodplain and monsoon vine-forest country.

What subgroup level means

A subgroup is a broad national class rather than a local community — it names the dominant structure and genus of the country (for example, tropical eucalypt woodlands with a tall annual grass understorey) rather than a garden-scale species list. Given how much of the Territory remains under native vegetation, that broad answer is often still a good guide to what belongs.

Toward a planting list
