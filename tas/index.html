Why Tasmania is different

Unlike the mainland states, Tasmania has never published a pre-1750 vegetation map. Its detailed statewide layer, TASVEG, maps vegetation as it is today, not as it was before clearing. So for the pre-European classification we read the national NVIS 7.0 pre-1750 layer.

TASVEG as present-day context

Tasmania retains a high proportion of its native vegetation, so in remnant bushland TASVEG is a strong guide to what belongs — and in cleared country it tells you what's there now. We show the TASVEG community for your point alongside the national pre-1750 answer, clearly labelled as present-day, so you can read the two together.

From here to a planting list

Tasmania's vegetation communities are described in detail in From Forest to Fjaeldmark. We're working those descriptions into indigenous planting lists — search your address and leave your email to be sent yours.
